About the college

A health-sciences college built around careers

Kasarani College of Medical and Health Science trains learners for roles in clinics, hospitals, laboratories and communities. Our programmes are TVET CDACC-accredited, and learners can join at one of five intakes each year.

Whether you are leaving secondary school or changing direction, we have a level that fits: diploma, certificate or artisan.

AccreditedTVET CDACC-accredited diploma, certificate and artisan programmes.
Flexible entryJoin in January, March, May, July or September.
Two campusesStudy in Nairobi or in Bomet Town.
Career focusCourses chosen for real roles in the health sector.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

When can I join?

We admit students in January, March, May, July and September.

Which levels do you offer?

Diploma, certificate and artisan programmes, all TVET CDACC-accredited.

Which campus should I choose?

You can study in Nairobi or Bomet. Programmes vary by campus, so call or WhatsApp us to confirm what is available.

What are the entry requirements and fees?

These depend on the course and level. Contact admissions on 0705 975 458 or 0711 106 757 for current details.

How do I apply?

Use the form below to send your details to our admissions team on WhatsApp, or call us directly.
